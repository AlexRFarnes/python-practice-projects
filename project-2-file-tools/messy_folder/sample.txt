The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The dog barks, but the fox runs away.

A quick fox is a clever fox, and a lazy dog is still a good dog. Foxes and dogs
don't always get along, but this fox and this dog are friends.

"The quick fox," said the dog, "you are quick, but I am quicker!"
The fox laughed and said, "We'll see about that, dog."

In the end, the fox and the dog raced across the field. The dog won, but only
just. The fox promised a rematch, and the dog agreed.
